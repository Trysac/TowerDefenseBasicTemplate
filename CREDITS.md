## Origin and removed content

The repository started in January 2022 as a copy of a Zenva tower defense course project
(commit "Full copy project Zenva"), then reorganized. The course material is no longer part of
the game: the `fix`/`feat` changes of 2026 removed its scripts, prefabs, ScriptableObjects and
demo scenes, the two tower models assembled for the course (`CannonTower.fbx`,
`CastleTower.fbx`) and their icon sprites, the TextMesh Pro essentials, and duplicate copies of
Kenney models. Those files remain in the git history, which was not rewritten.
